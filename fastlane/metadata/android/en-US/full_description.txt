Track your time with Toggl Track right from your wrist.

Track Watch is a Wear OS app with a companion phone app for Toggl Track users:

• Start a favorite or one of your most frequent timers with one tap
• See the running timer, stop it, cancel it, or move its start time with the crown
• Browse the last 7 days of history with daily totals; continue, edit or delete entries
• Tile with your timers and an Open button, a watch-face complication with the elapsed time, and the running timer on your watch face
• Works offline: actions are queued and synced when your phone and Toggl are reachable
• Shows how many Toggl API requests you have left this hour

Install both apps: the watch app needs the Track Watch phone app on the paired phone, which does the syncing with Toggl. On the phone you paste your Toggl Track API token once and set up your favorites. Your token stays encrypted on your phone; the app talks only to Toggl's API. No ads, no tracking.

Track Watch is open source (MIT): https://github.com/ihoru/toggl-track-watch
