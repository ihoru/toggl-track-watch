### Added
- Watch: the running-timer chip on the watch face starts with how long the timer has been running,
  in whole minutes ("15m reading a book", "1h 5m reading a book"); it updates once a minute.
  Settings shows **Update every minute** when the watch doesn't yet allow the app's alarms
  (Alarms & reminders); without that, Android may update the chip only every ~10 minutes.
